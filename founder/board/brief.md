# Board brief: Đèn ngủ thú cưng – XG_Studio

(Everything below comes from the founder's own project files. Prices in VND; 250.000 VND ≈ 10 USD.)

## The idea
Shop name: XG_Studio. Slogan: "Decor độc · đẹp · thông minh" (unique · beautiful · smart decor).
A layered, laser-cut wooden night light showing a night-time window scene. The moon is a working clock face. Personalised to the buyer's pet. Two designs so far:

**Bản Mực (dog version)** – 8 layers of 3 mm wood, 18.5 × 16.7 cm. Front to back:
1. Dog face with floppy ears, the pet's name, a collar whose name tag hides the motion-sensor "eye", 2 cat-tail key hooks
2. Curtains, with a keepsake bell on the cord that also holds the key hooks
3. Window sill with the dog, a tennis ball, a food bowl with a bone
4. Arched window frame
5. Near hills and pine trees
6. Valley mist
7. Ninh Bình limestone mountains
8. Sky: the moon as it looked on the night the pet came home, used as the clock face; 12 stars as hour marks; a glowing dashed bone
Behind: milky-white acrylic with hidden paw prints that appear when lit; spacer frames holding a clock movement, LED strip and sensor circuit; a back panel with a message, a QR code (to a memorial web page), 2 wall-hanging holes, 4 screws to change the battery.
Functions: night light, real clock on the moon, keepsake picture, key holder, lights up by itself when someone comes home (motion sensor).

**Bản Mướp (cat version)** – 15 × 15 cm, framed as a "đèn kỷ niệm" (memorial/keepsake lamp). Front frame with name, homecoming date and 2 sliders "breakfast / dinner" (a feeding tracker); curtains with hidden slider track; sill with cat and plant pot; arched window; far hills and pines; sky with the moon on the homecoming night and the Pisces constellation; milky acrylic with hidden paw prints; spacers for LED; back panel with message and QR to a memorial page.

## Status
- Both 3D designs are finished, built from the actual laser-cut geometry. There are interactive 3D web pages (rotate, switch the light on, explode the layers, view the back) and rendered posters (day and night).
- No laser cutter / no 3D printer yet. No physical prototype has been made.
- Laser-cut export files (DXF/SVG per layer) are not exported yet.

## The plan the founder has decided
1. First, sell the design files + 3D images on Cults3D, Etsy and MakerWorld to see how people react. File price not yet set.
2. Then decide whether to make physical lamps. Target mass-market price for the physical lamp: about 250.000 VND.
The founder's own bar: the product must be unusual, touch emotions, and give a clear reason to buy.

## Not known yet (do not assume)
- The specific target customer (age, income, Vietnam vs. international).
- Cost to produce one physical lamp (wood, acrylic, clock movement, LED, sensor, battery, labour, packaging, personalisation time).
- Where physical lamps would be sold, and how personalisation orders would be taken.
- Budget, competitors, market size, customer acquisition cost.
